What is Ertlanner?

Ertlanner is a desktop planner for students and young professionals, on Windows and macOS. It puts a weekly class schedule, a focus timer and study history in one place. At university level it also keeps a CV and transcript archive, internship applications and projects.

  • Weekly class schedule, with meetings from Google Calendar
  • Focus timer and stopwatch, with history and statistics
  • Collectible cards: every finished session of 10 minutes or more earns an illustrated card. Its tier (Wood, Bronze, Silver or Gold) comes from the session length, and Silver and Gold sessions have a small chance to turn into Diamond or Titanium.
  • Study log: questions solved per subject each day, plus photos of the ones left unsolved
  • Optional Team and Friends: share schedules and study logs with people you invite

Your data

Data is stored on your computer. If you sign in with Google, Ertlanner syncs your devices through a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. What you share in Team and Friends is stored in Google Firebase. The privacy policy has the details.

Download

Installers for Windows and macOS are on the releases page. Installed copies update themselves from signed releases.

How it is built

Electron, React, TypeScript and SQLite on the desktop, Firebase (Firestore) for Team and Friends, and GitHub Actions to test, build and sign every release. An iPhone and iPad version is in progress.
