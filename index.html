What is Ertlanner?

Ertlanner is a desktop planner for students. It runs on Windows and macOS and keeps your class schedule, focus sessions and study history in one place. University students can also keep their CV, transcripts, internship applications and projects in it.

  • Weekly class schedule with your Google Calendar meetings
  • Focus timer and stopwatch with history and statistics
  • A collectible card for every finished session of 10 minutes or more
  • Longer sessions earn better cards. Silver and Gold sessions can rarely turn into Diamond or Titanium.
  • Study log for the questions you solve each day
  • Team and Friends to share your schedule with people you invite

Your data

Your data stays on your computer. If you sign in with Google, your devices sync through a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. Anything you share in Team and Friends is stored in Google Firebase. The privacy policy has the details.

Download

Windows and macOS installers are on the releases page. The app updates itself after that.

How it is built

The desktop app uses Electron, React, TypeScript and SQLite. Team and Friends run on Firebase. GitHub Actions tests, builds and signs every release. An iPhone and iPad version is on the way.
