Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Summary

Ertlanner is a desktop application. Your data stays on your computer and, if you turn on sync, only in your own Google account's Google Drive storage. The developer cannot access this data and does not collect, sell or share it.

If you turn on the optional Team or Friends feature, the information you choose to share is stored in Google Firebase and is visible only to members of your team or to friends you have mutually added. When you invite or add someone, the access record given to them also contains your address, and only that person can see it. All other data stays on your computer and in your own Google Drive.

Which Google data is accessed?

  • Basic account info (openid, email): to show which account is signed in and to make sure synced data belongs to that account.
  • Google Drive app data folder (drive.appdata): to sync Ertlanner data between your own devices, using only the hidden app folder that Ertlanner creates. Ertlanner cannot see or modify any other files in your Drive.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.calendarlist.readonly, calendar.events.readonly): optionally, to show your meetings next to your class schedule. Nothing is added to or changed in your calendar.

Where is data stored?

Study records, schedule, documents and application information are stored in the app's data folder on your computer. If sync is on, a copy is written to the hidden Ertlanner app folder in your own Google Drive. Google sign-in credentials are encrypted with the operating system's secure storage (Windows user-account encryption, macOS Keychain) and stored only on your computer.

Your Çalışma Günlüğü (Study Log) entries, the photos you add to Yapamadığım Sorular (Unsolved Questions), your Günü Bitir (End the Day) ratings and the cards in Koleksiyonum (My Collection) are kept in the same data folder; if sync is on, they are copied only to your own Drive. Cards are derived on your computer from the length of the focus and stopwatch sessions you complete and a random number; a lucky upgrade to Elmas (Diamond) or Titanyum (Titanium) is also decided by that number. Each card is a short code stored with that session (collection, tier and the number that picks the card), and no separate server is contacted for cards. Your End the Day ratings are never sent to your team, your coach, your friends or the developer; there is no way to share them. Your answer on the launch screen that asks whether you are ready is not saved or sent anywhere.

Your Kullanıcı Düzeyi (User Level) and the sections you turn off in Ayarlar > Görünen Bölümler (Settings > Visible Sections) are stored on your computer and, if sync is on, copied to your own Drive so that all your computers look the same; these choices are not sent to your team, your friends or the developer. Turning a section off only changes what you see: no data is deleted, and your active sharing continues even while Ekip (Team) or Arkadaşlar (Friends) is hidden.

Team (Optional)

The Team feature works only if you turn it on. Creating a team requires access (see Invite-Based Access below); joining one does not. For teams, the following is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore in Europe; Firebase Authentication in the US): your Google account ID and email address, the display name you choose, your team membership and invitations, the class schedule you choose to share (the last 4 weeks, this week and next week: course name, day, time, duration, color; classes you hide from the team are excluded), and the messages and feedback you write.

Locations, Google Calendar events, projects, internship applications, documents and session notes are never shared. Only members of your team can see this information; invited people also see the team name, the owner's name and the email address of everyone in the team (including pending invitees). As owner of the Firebase project, the developer can technically access it but uses it only to operate the service, troubleshoot and prevent abuse. Google operates Firebase as a data processor under the Firebase Data Processing Terms.

If you turn on study log sharing, the following is also stored in Firebase and seen by your team (including the team owner and, if labeled, your coach): your log entries for the last 4 weeks and this week (subject name or only its color, question count, topic, note), your study time totals per day and per subject, the number of sessions you completed, your daily goal and on how many days you met it, and the number of cards you earned that week per tier. While Ders Adlarını Göster (Show Subject Names) is off, subject names are not sent and the team sees only subject colors.

In Unsolved Questions, only the questions you share one by one with Ekiple Paylaş (Share with Team) reach your team, and only while study log sharing is on. For each shared question, a downscaled photo (at most 350 KB) and a small preview, its subject (name or color), topic, note, the day you added it and whether it is solved are stored in Firebase. The photo is downscaled on your computer before it is shared; at most 30 questions can be shared at a time. If a photo shows your name, your school or other personal information, crop it or do not share it.

In Yapılacaklar (To-dos), only the to-dos you share one by one with Ekiple Paylaş (Share with Team) reach your team. The switch is off by default. For each shared to-do, its title, due day (and time, if set), whether it is done and the color of its subject are stored in Firebase. The subject name is sent only while Ders Adlarını Göster (Show Subject Names) is on. Notes, reminders and settings that belong to your computer are never sent. Your team sees only the current week: open to-dos and the ones you completed this week. A shared to-do goes only to the team you were in when you shared it.

The team owner may label members as Koç (Coach); the label is visual only and grants no rights. Everyone in the team sees the log and questions you share and the feedback written about them. Your streak, session notes, the cards themselves and your End the Day ratings are never sent to the team.

Friends (Optional)

Friends is meant for the Okul (School) view and is hidden in the Üniversite Ve Profesyonel (University and Professional) view. It works only for accounts with access (see Invite-Based Access below). It starts only when you add an address, and a friendship exists only when both sides add each other's email address. Nobody can send you a request, and there is no public profile, search or chat. An address you add is kept as a pending add until that person adds you too or you cancel it; this record is readable only by you and the address you added, and Ertlanner does not show it to the other side.

Firebase stores your friendships (both sides' account IDs and email addresses) and your friend profile. Your friends see: your email address, your display name, this week's Focus Score with its parts (focus time points, days studied, cards earned and the highest tier) and, if Koleksiyonumu Paylaş (Share My Collection) is on, your collection summary. The collection summary is the list of your card codes (newest first, at most about 40,000 characters); your card counts, tier distribution, best cards and album completion derive from it, and it contains no subject names or dates. If Odak Puanımı Paylaş (Share My Focus Score) is off, your score is not sent. Your study log, notes, questions, subject names, streak, End the Day ratings and team messages never reach friends.

The Focus Score (Odak Puanı) is calculated on your computer, ranges from 0 to 100 and starts from zero every Monday: 1 point for every 10 minutes of focus or stopwatch time (at most 60), 5 points for every day with at least 10 minutes of study (at most 35) and 1 point for every card earned (at most 5). Manually added sessions and question counts from the log do not count. The per-day breakdown, the comparison with last week and your streak appear only on your own card; friend cards are listed by name, with no ranking or winner.

Invite-Based Access

Creating a team and using Friends require access; being invited to a team and joining one do not. Access starts with the accounts on a list that the developer manages by hand. The list is kept in Firebase by email address, and the app cannot read it. When someone with access invites an address to their team or adds it as a friend, that address usually gets access too, and an access record is written to Firebase. Once the team owner has confirmed this, the owner's Ertlanner also writes this record for members and pending invitees already in the team.

An access record contains: the address that received access, the account ID and email address of the person who gave it, how it was given (team invitation or friend add; for a team invitation, the team's ID), when it was written, and a chain of at most three account IDs. The chain shows which accounts the access passed through, from the listed account to the person who gave it; it contains no email addresses or names. Access reaches at most three links from a listed account: an account on the third link can create a team and invite people but cannot give anyone access. Only the owner of the address, and the developer as owner of the Firebase project, can read the record; the person who gave access cannot read, change or delete it.

An account can give access to at most 10 addresses a day, and a team owner can send at most 20 new invitations a day. For these limits, two daily counters are kept for each account that invites or gives access. The counters are named with a code derived from your address, do not contain the address itself and hold only the day, that day's count and the ID of the latest record (a code derived from the latest address given access, or the latest invitation's team). Only you can read your counters; they cannot be deleted from the app.

In case of abuse, the developer can block an account; blocked addresses and account IDs are kept in the same list. A blocked account cannot create a team, use Friends, invite anyone, join a team, write messages, share questions or study log weeks or turn on new sharing (a class schedule share that was already on continues until the account is removed from the team). A blocked address cannot be invited or added as a friend. Access given by the blocked account, and access passed on further down the chain from it, stops counting too; someone with access can give such an account (if it is not blocked itself) access again by inviting or adding it.

Team and Friends Data Retention and Deletion

  • A shared week (class schedule or study log) is hidden from the team about 4 weeks after it ends and is deleted when your or the team owner's Ertlanner runs.
  • A shared question is deleted from the team 7 days after you mark it solved, when you unshare it or when you delete the question. For an unsolved question you keep shared, your Ertlanner extends its lifetime about once a month; if your Ertlanner does not run for 35 days, the question becomes invisible to the team and is deleted when your Ertlanner runs again.
  • Shared to-dos are visible to the team only for the current week. Last week's list is deleted when your Ertlanner runs in the new week. A to-do also leaves the team when you unshare it, delete it or leave the team.
  • Messages are deleted after 180 days when the author's or the team owner's Ertlanner runs.
  • Team invitations remain until the owner withdraws them or the invited person declines.
  • When you turn sharing off, those weeks are deleted immediately; turning study log sharing off also deletes the questions you shared.
  • When you leave a team, your shared schedule, log and questions and the feedback written about you are deleted, and optionally your messages.
  • If the team owner removes you, your shared schedule, log and questions, your messages and the feedback written about you are deleted.
  • "Ekip Verilerimi Sil" (Delete My Team Data) on the Ekip page makes you leave your current team (if you own it, the team is deleted for everyone); your shared schedule, log, questions and to-dos, your messages and what was written about you are deleted, every Share with Team switch in To-dos is turned off, your friendships, pending adds, friend profile and the access record given to you are removed, and your team account is closed. The access records you gave to others (their address, your address and your account ID), your account ID in records passed on through them and your daily counters remain. Messages you left in a team you had already left are not covered; to have them deleted, write to the contact address below.
  • A pending add remains until the person adds you too, you cancel it or you delete your friends data; when a friendship forms, both pending records are deleted.
  • A friendship remains until either side chooses Arkadaşlıktan Çıkar (Remove Friend) or deletes their friends data; once it ends, the other side can no longer read your profile.
  • Your friend profile is overwritten every week and whenever it changes; if it is not renewed within 35 days, it becomes invisible to your friends. Turning off Share My Focus Score or Share My Collection removes that part from your profile.
  • If the developer blocks an account, or the account loses access for another reason, it can no longer use Friends, and its profile becomes invisible to friends within 35 days, or immediately if its account ID is blocked. To have the remaining records deleted, write to the contact address below.
  • "Arkadaş Verilerimi Sil" (Delete My Friends Data) on the Arkadaşlar page deletes all your friendships, your pending adds (including those addressed to you) and your profile. Access records, your team and the records on your computer are not affected.
  • An access record remains until the owner of the address uses "Ekip Verilerimi Sil" or Delete My Account. Withdrawing an invitation, removing a member, cancelling an add or removing a friend does not delete the record or take the access away, and the person who gave it cannot delete it. To have a record for a mistyped address or an address that does not use Ertlanner deleted, write to the contact address below.
  • Daily counters cannot be deleted from the app; they are overwritten on each new day.

Update Check

To find out whether a new version exists, Ertlanner connects to the public ertlanner-releases page on github.com. No personal data is sent with this request; GitHub sees the connection's IP address.

Sharing

Ertlanner communicates only with Google services (Google Account, Drive, Calendar, Firebase) and with GitHub to check for updates. Data is not sold or used for advertising or analytics. In the Team feature, only the information you choose is shown to your team members; in Friends, only your score and, if you choose, your collection summary are shown to friends you have mutually added. A person you invite or add can see your address and account ID in the access record given to them.

Revoking Access and Deleting Data

You can revoke Ertlanner's access at any time on your Google Account permissions page. You can delete Ertlanner's data in Drive via Google Drive > Settings > Manage apps > Ertlanner > Delete hidden app data. For team and friends data, use "Ekip Verilerimi Sil" on the Ekip page first (or, for friends data only, "Arkadaş Verilerimi Sil" on the Arkadaşlar page); revoking Google access does not delete your data in Firebase by itself. Data on your computer is removed by deleting the app's data folder.

To delete your account from inside the app, use Ayarlar > Hesap Ve Eşitleme > Hesabımı Sil (Settings > Account and Sync > Delete My Account). It deletes your team account and your data in Firebase with the same scope as "Ekip Verilerimi Sil" (your shares, messages and what was written about you in your team, your friendships, pending adds, friend profile and the access record given to you; if you own the team, it is deleted for everyone), revokes Ertlanner's permission in your Google account and turns off sync. It needs an internet connection; if the permission cannot be revoked, you can remove it on the Google Account permissions page. Delete My Account does not delete the records and documents on your computer, the hidden Ertlanner folder in your Google Drive, the access records you gave to others and your account ID in records passed on through them, or your daily counters. If you never accepted the Ekip Özelliği (Team Feature) notice, nothing is deleted in Firebase on your behalf; if someone invited you to their team or added you as a friend, that invitation and the access record given to you remain, and you can ask for their deletion at the contact address below. You can remove the Drive copy through the Google Drive path above, and the data on your computer by deleting the app's data folder while Ertlanner is closed. Ayarlar > Veriler Ve Yedekler > Veri Klasörünü Aç (Settings > Data and Backups > Open Data Folder) shows the folder; in an installed copy it is %APPDATA%\Ertlanner on Windows and ~/Library/Application Support/Ertlanner on macOS.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Ertlanner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google Calendar data is not shared through the Team or Friends features.
